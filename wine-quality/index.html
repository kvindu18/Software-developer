Methodology

End-to-end workflow

1. Prepare

Inspect the dataset, handle missing/duplicate observations, remove Id, and separate quality from the 11 chemical inputs.

2. Train

Use an 80/20 stratified split with random_state 42. Scale features where appropriate.

3. Evaluate

Compare Logistic Regression, Random Forest, SVM and KNN, then use tuning/cross-validation for model improvement.
